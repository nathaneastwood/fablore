Legendary Stories

Lore Graph

Every story on this site is joined to the heroes, characters, places, creatures and items it records. This is that record drawn as a map: each dot is a story or an entity, each line is an appearance.

It opens on the sets, their stories, and the heroes and regions running through them; drawing everything at once buries the shape you came to see. Characters, places, creatures and items are each one tap away below, beside a slider for how well connected a thing must be to appear and a search box for jumping straight to a name you already know. Choose anything to read what it connects to and to open its page. On a narrow screen the map becomes a ranked list, which is what a phone has the room for.

    Hover a node to see its connections. Click to open its page. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom. In the panel, a name opens its page and the target button beside it moves the graph to that connection. A solid line means an appearance in a story; a dashed one joins a hero, weapon or piece of equipment to a set its card was printed in, which is not the same thing.

    Tap an entry to read its connections, most connected first. The number beside a name is how many connections it has.
