The infernal colossus that is Baalghor manifested as an embodiment of the Shadow Aesir’s eldritch resilience. During the Second Age, when Shadow and Light fought incessantly all across Rathe, Baalghor was created to guard the realm of its besieged Aesir.

Baalghor’s demonic duty was to smite any radiant enemies that dared to intrude. Wielding annihilative power harvested from the aether of the Shadowrealm itself, this behemoth’s propensity for obliteration was unprecedented.

Banished with its creator to i’Arathael, Baalghor’s purpose is now to guard the Abyss, the most sacred site in the Shadowrealm. Any Demonastery expeditions that have dared explore the outer reaches of the Abyss have never returned.

There is only one consequence of entering Baalghor’s awareness: certain doom.
