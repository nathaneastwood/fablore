# Dr. Mortimer, Blight of the Pits

:::hero-blurb
The demented Dr. Mortimer treats the Pits like his petri dish, infecting unwitting subjects to extract payment for his “miraculous” cures.
:::

A doctor to some, a blight to all. Earning his title through experimentation on the discarded patients of Southmaw Asylum, Dr. Mortimer learned firsthand what makes the mortal machine tick… and what makes it stop. Long before he took up any dagger or contract, the doctor honed his skills with both scalpel and codex, studying the writings of the cult known as L’Apocalypta.

The cult’s fascination with disease captivated Dr. Mortimer and, as infection bloomed throughout the Pits, drew him out of the Asylum. His lab work became field research. His patients? Any and all who sought his cures. Each ailment posed new questions to be answered. New weaknesses to pick apart. To study. Word of the doctor spread as virally as the diseases.

Until one day, Dr. Mortimer vanished. His name now uttered in hushed tones by the sick. Perhaps, out of hope. Out of reverence.

Out of fear.
