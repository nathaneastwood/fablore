When Killjoy steps into the Deathmatch Arena, opponents and spectators alike clutch their valuables to their chests. This cocky Warrior’s crooked blade is infamous for slashing throats and stealing treasures. Word of Killjoy’s penchant for pinching has traveled far and wide, and furious cries erupt from the stands at the mere sight of her!

But does that weigh upon this wild Warrior? No more than dust upon a blade!

The origins of Killjoy are a mystery, but it is clear to all that she has a perverse love of being despised. With a chip on her shoulder the size of Rathe itself, Killjoy revels in the crowd’s hatred, deliberately whipping them into a furious frenzy as she plunders the bodies of her fallen competitors.

Every cry of derision only widens her grin and lends cruel strength to the swings of her blade. The louder the crowd demands her downfall, the harder she strikes, leaving shattered opponents in her wake as she carves her way to yet another Deathmatch victory.

To Killjoy, hatred is not a burden, it is the most potent fuel a Warrior could ask for!
