# Zane, Broadly Beloved

:::hero-blurb
Delighting the Deathmatch Arena with feats of strength and commanding charisma, Zane swings massive swords with ease and alacrity. Even his opponents can’t help but join in the cheers!
:::

*Come one, come all!* Zane is bound for the arena, so you’d best claim a seat before they’re gone!

Fans travel far and wide to witness this hefty hero battle in Deathmatch. His prodigious strength allows him to wield not one, but TWO massive weapons. That alone is said to be a feat worthy of the history books – but is his power the sole cause of his popularity? Not even close!

Zane’s larger than life personality wins him friends wherever he goes. Good-hearted and born with the gift of the gab, Zane never fails to banter with the Deathmatch crowd. He even wagers with his opponents when he’ll land his next mighty blow, and when he’s right, the captivated crowd whoops and hollers at the sight of it! Zane’s actions fill the arena with such good cheer that each competitor feels their strength swell within. Who *wouldn’t* love a Warrior whose very presence lifts the spirits of all?

*Move swiftly, now!* Zane, Broadly Beloved is ready to swing his swords, and an arena this merry is not to be missed!
