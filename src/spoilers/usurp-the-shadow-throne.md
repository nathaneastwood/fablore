# Usurp the Shadow Throne

Legendary Stories has been given two cards from Usurp the Shadow Throne to reveal. Here they are.

<img src="https://d2hl7maqck52px.cloudfront.net/spoiler/usurp-the-shadow-throne.webp" alt="Countdown to Extinction and Darkest Hour, from Usurp the Shadow Throne" class="center" />

Both are illustrated by Nailsen Ivanderlie.

## Countdown to Extinction

A three-cost Shadow Attack Action, six power, IAR167.

> When this attacks, create a Gate to i'Arathael token.
>
> When this hits, you may search your deck for a Darkest Hour, banish it, then shuffle.
>
> **Blood Debt** (while this is banished, at the beginning of your end phase, lose 1 life).

## Darkest Hour

A two-cost Shadow Action, IAR209.

> You may put a card from your hand on top of your deck rather than pay this card's cost.
>
> Your next Shadow attack this turn gets +4 power. **Go again**
>
> **Blood Debt** — while this is banished, at the beginning of your end phase, lose 1 life.
